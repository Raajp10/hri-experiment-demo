Delivery Robot Study
Information and consent
Research study

Consent to take part in a research study

Study: Trust in delivery robots (human–robot interaction research, Purdue University Northwest).

What you will do: answer a few background questions, watch short videos of simulated delivery robots navigating a warehouse, make decisions about where the robots should go, and rate the robots. The study takes about 15–20 minutes.

Risks and benefits: there are no known risks beyond those of normal computer use. You may not benefit directly, but you will help us understand how people work with robots.

Voluntary participation: you may stop at any time by closing this window, without penalty.

Data we collect: we collect only non-identifiable data: your answers to questions, your choices and ratings during the study, and response times. We do not collect your name, email, or any other information that could identify you. Your responses are stored under an anonymous ID and reported only in aggregate.

Contact: questions about the study can be sent to the researcher.

By clicking "I agree" you confirm that you are at least 18 years old, have read this information, and agree to take part.
