What the participant does

  1. Consent: says that only non-identifiable data is collected
  2. About you: age (18–80), gender, used PsiTurk before (Y/N), worked with robots before (Y/N) questiondata
  3. Attitudes toward robots (NARS, with an attention check) questiondata
  4. Sound check: the robots speak in the videos
  5. Training video and a practice delivery
  6. Robot 1: instructions for its condition with a screenshot, then 3 deliveries trialdata
  7. Questionnaire about Robot 1 (MDMT, reliance, manipulation checks) questiondata
  8. Robot 2: the other autonomy level, 3 deliveries trialdata, questionnaire questiondata
  9. Open-ended question, then the debriefing questiondata

A countdown timer in the header is shown on every page.

The four conditions

Reliability (between participants): the reliable robots are always right; the unreliable robots are wrong on deliveries 1 and 3.

Autonomy (within participants, order counterbalanced):

  • Low: the robot asks "I suggest going to Room X. Confirm?" and the participant confirms or chooses the other room.
  • Mid: the robot decides on its own and reports back. The participant answers "Did the robot reach the correct room?"

After every delivery: "How much do you trust the robot right now?" (1–7).
